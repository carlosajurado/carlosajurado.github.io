Upper Limits on Stellar Companions in Kepler Circumbinary Systems

Circumbinary systems are systems with planets orbiting both stars in a binary. We used new spectra of Kepler-34 and Kepler-35 to search for long-period stellar companions. We modeled a constant acceleration and used MCMC to place upper limits on companion mass.

The Effects of Natal Kicks in The Galactic Center

Natal kicks are velocity impulses received by neutron stars or black holes at birth. We simulated their effect on a population of massive stars and binaries near the Galactic Center.

About Me

I’m an Astronomy graduate student at the University of Texas at Austin working in Keith Hawkins’s research group on the origins of azimuthal metallicity variations. I have broad researh interests ranging from galactic dynamics, stellar chemistry & dynamics, and exoplanetary systems.

I graduated with a B.S. in Astrophysics from UCLA and while at UCLA, I worked with Prof. Smadar Naoz on stellar and compact object dynamics in the Galactic Center. I also had the opportunity to work with Prof. Lauren Weiss at Notre Dame to search for stellar companions in Kepler circumbinary systems.
